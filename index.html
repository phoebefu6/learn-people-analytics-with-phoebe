Learn People Analytics with Phoebe

People analytics has a reputation for being soft and is the opposite: every number describes a person who can be counted exactly, so there is no excuse for a rate without its base or a table nobody asked "who is missing" of. These sixteen sessions teach the discipline twice: once as habits anyone can use with no code, on a constructed company whose 210 leavers give three defensible attrition rates, and once as numbers you compute, on 11,301 real employers' pay gap filings. The running finding: at the median employer most of the reported gap is the pyramid, women being 56 percent of the bottom pay band and 40 of the top, and the lever every well-meaning plan reaches for, hire more women, widens the gap when they are hired at the bottom. The bench in the last session fits the ladder live and lets you pull that lever both ways.
