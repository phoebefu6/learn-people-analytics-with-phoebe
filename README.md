# Learn People Analytics with Phoebe

People analytics for data and AI teams. Two tracks: a six-session **Foundations** track with no
code (headcount and flows, attrition and its base, the hiring funnel, pay gaps and fairness,
surveys as evidence, the people dashboard) and a ten-session **Data and AI deep-dive** in Python
(the people data model, attrition as survival, regretted and benchmarked, funnel maths, pay gap
decomposition on 11,301 real employers, pay equity regression, survey psychometrics, workforce
planning, AI on people data and what the law forbids, and a bench).

The pay gap spine is the UK gender pay gap service's 2024 filings (Open Government Licence v3.0).
The running company, Apex, is constructed. The bench recomputes the pyramid, the fitted band
ladder and the anti-lever live in the browser on 3,000 real employers.

Live: https://phoebefu6.github.io/learn-people-analytics-with-phoebe/

Static HTML, CSS and JS. No build step. `materials/build-paygap.py` rebuilds every filing number,
the bench sample and the JOLTS series; `materials/build-apex.py` rebuilds every Apex number.
